# Consent Form: Webcam Hand-Gesture Recognition Study

## Study Overview
This study develops a hand-gesture recognition system to replace the trackpad for computer control. You will record hand landmarks (joint positions, not video) during controlled gesture sessions and continuous usage.

## What You'll Do
- Record 8 gestures, 10 repeats each, in 2 lighting conditions (~20 min)
- Participate in 3–4 continuous sessions mimicking natural browsing with interspersed gestures (~15 min each)
- Optionally: participate in a user study comparing gestures vs trackpad (30–45 min)

## Data Collected
- **Landmarks only:** 21 (x, y, z) positions per frame (~30 FPS). No video, audio, or personal information.
- **Metadata:** Anonymous subject ID, session date/time, lighting condition, hand size, handedness, skin tone (optional).
- **No recording of:** Video, audio, screen content, keystroke content, or any identifying information.

## Data Storage and Release
- Raw landmarks stored locally during the study, encrypted at rest.
- Dataset released publicly as **landmarks only** under [CC-BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with a datasheet describing data collection, labelling, and known biases.
- You may opt in to raw video release (for your session only) for full transparency; video is kept private otherwise.
- All data deleted 12 months after study end, except the consented landmark dataset.

## Your Rights
- You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
- You may refuse specific recordings (e.g. "not today" or "stop recording my video").
- The researcher will not use your data for any purpose other than this project and its public release.
- You will not be identified in any reports or media; your anonymous ID is your link to your data.

## Risks and Benefits
- **Risks:** Minimal. Webcam use, brief hand fatigue, arm position discomfort (you choose chair/table setup).
- **Benefits:** None direct to you. Your participation helps evaluate a new input method and contributes to accessibility research.
